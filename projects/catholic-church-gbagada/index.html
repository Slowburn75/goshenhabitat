Project Overview

The Our Lady Queen of Peace Catholic Church project involved the design and construction of a new worship center in Gbagada, Lagos. The design emphasizes natural light, acoustics, and a solemn atmosphere appropriate for worship.

The structure was built to accommodate a large congregation while maintaining a sense of community and reverence.

02

Challenges

  • Site Constraints: Maximizing the auditorium capacity within a confined urban site.
  • Timing: Coordinating construction activities around church schedules and community events.
03

Outcome

A landmark religious edifice that serves as a spiritual home for thousands of parishioners.
