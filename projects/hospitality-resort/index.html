Royal Cedar Hotel

AJIWE, AJAH, LAGOS STATEArchitectural Design Civil Engineering Landscape Design

01

Project Overview

Royal Cedar Hotel is a luxury resort featuring 150 guest rooms, private villas, a spa, and multiple dining venues. The design concept draws inspiration from the local coastal landscape, using natural materials and organic forms. We provided full architectural and engineering services for this expansive project.

The resort is designed to be eco-friendly, with solar power integration, rainwater harvesting, and a waste management system. It offers guests a seamless blend of modern comfort and natural beauty.

02

Challenges

  • Environmental Impact: Minimizing disruption to the local ecosystem during construction.
  • Remote Location: Transporting materials and workforce to the coastal site.
  • Weather: Managing construction schedules during the rainy season.
03

Outcome

The resort opened to rave reviews and has become a top destination for luxury travelers. It has set a benchmark for sustainable tourism development in the region.
