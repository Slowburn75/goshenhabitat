Mowe Residence

Mowe, Ogun StateArchitectural Design Construction

Project Overview

This project involved the design and construction of a private residence in Mowe, Ogun State. The brief called for a modern, functional home that maximizes family living spaces while maintaining privacy.

We focused on creating a design that is both aesthetically pleasing and structurally sound, ensuring use of quality materials and efficient spatial planning.

02

Challenges

  • Site Topography: Adapting the level design to the natural terrain of the site.
  • Material Sourcing: Ensuring timely delivery of high-quality materials to the Mowe location.
03

Outcome

A beautifully finished residence that meets the client's vision for a modern family home.
