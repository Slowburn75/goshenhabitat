Past & Present Projects

A chronological record of our completed and ongoing architectural, construction, and consultancy projects across Nigeria.

2006

Remodelling of FCMB Calabar Branch, Calabar Road, Cross River State.

2007
  • Royal Cedar Hotel, opposite Fidelity Bank, Ajiwe, Ajah, Lagos (with Newform Concept).
  • Satellite Microfinance Bank, Ikota Branch, Lagos (Newform Concept).
2009

Construction of FCMB Branch, Murtala Mohammed Way, Ilorin, Kwara State (Isthmus Marginal Services).

2010
  • Design and construction of House of Grace Fashion House, opposite Big Treat Eatery, Omole Junction, Agidingbi, Ikeja, Lagos.
  • Construction of church auditoriums for RCCG Love & Faith Pavilion Parishes, Akute, Ogun State.
2011

Design and construction of office complex at Taiwo Street, off Kudirat Abiola Way, Alausa, Ikeja, Lagos.

2012
  • Construction of Professional Insurance Ladies Association (PILA) Secretariat Building, Iwaya, Yaba, Lagos.
  • Design and construction of residential project at Lubcon Road, Ilorin, Kwara State.
2013
  • Design and construction of residential building at Olusegun Olusemoh Street, Parkview Estate, Ikoyi, Lagos.
  • Construction of church auditorium for Anglican Church, Iragberi, Osun State.
2014
  • Design and construction of 6-bedroom country home residential building, Sobe, Edo State.
  • Re-modelling of Rightway House (Royal Event Hall), Balogun Bus Stop, Awolowo Way, Ikeja, Lagos.
  • Design and construction of Royal Cedar Hotel Phase II, Ajiwe, Ajah, Lagos.
2015
  • Construction of 3D Event Centre, Ketu, Lagos.
  • Construction of permanent church building for Our Lady Queen of Peace Catholic Church, Gbagada, Lagos (under construction).
  • Consultancy and supervision of block of 12 units, 3-bedroom luxury apartments, Oniru, Lagos for NEM Insurance (ongoing).
