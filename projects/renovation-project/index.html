Remodeling

Rightway House

Lagos, NigeriaBuilding Remodeling Event Space Design Structural & MEP Upgrades Interior Finishing

01

Project Overview

This project involved the comprehensive remodeling of an existing building into a fully functional, modern event center designed to host social, corporate, and ceremonial gatherings. The objective was to transform the aging structure into a versatile, high-capacity venue while meeting contemporary safety, comfort, and aesthetic standards.

Our scope of work included spatial reconfiguration, structural upgrades, interior finishing, and the installation of modern electrical, lighting, and climate control systems. The result is a flexible event space capable of accommodating a wide range of functions.

02

Challenges

  • Structural Adaptation: Modifying the existing structure to support large crowd loads and open-span event spaces.
  • Acoustic Control: Designing sound insulation and acoustics suitable for events without disturbing surrounding properties.
  • Modern Compliance: Upgrading the facility to meet current fire safety, accessibility, and building regulations.
03

Outcome

The remodeled facility was successfully delivered as a modern event center with enhanced capacity, improved aesthetics, and optimized functionality. The space now supports weddings, conferences, exhibitions, and corporate events, significantly increasing the commercial value and usability of the property.
