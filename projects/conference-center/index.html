Conference Center

Ilawe-Ekiti, Ekiti StateDesign & Build Construction Management Structural Engineering

01

Project Overview

The Ongoing Conference Center project at Ilawe-Ekiti in Ekiti State is a landmark development aimed at providing a world-class venue for conferences, conventions, and social events. Designed with modern architectural principles, the center will feature spacious auditoriums, meeting rooms, and banquet halls.

This project underscores our commitment to delivering high-quality institutional and commercial infrastructure that fosters community engagement and economic growth.

02

Key Features

  • Capacity: Large capacity main auditorium and multiple breakout rooms.
  • Technology: Integrated state-of-the-art audio-visual systems.
  • Sustainability: Energy-efficient lighting and cooling systems.
03

Status

The project is currently ongoing, with structural works progressing according to schedule. We are maintaining strict quality control and safety standards throughout the construction phase.
